# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

As maintainers, contributors, and users of **KiloOS**, we pledge to foster an open, professional, and rigorous environment dedicated to bare-metal systems engineering and pure Assembly development. We respect all individuals who contribute to our low-level mission, provided they adhere to our engineering standards.

## Standards of Behavior

* **Technical Merit First:** Discussions must revolve around architecture, registers, memory maps, UEFI protocols, and pure Assembly code optimization.
* **Professional Conduct:** Harassment, discrimination, or disruptive behavior of any kind will not be tolerated. Keep communications respectful and focused on technical excellence.
* **Adherence to Governance:** The BDFL model (João / dev12124) holds final authority on architectural and governance decisions. Respect the review process and the maintainer hierarchy outlined in `CONTRIBUTORS.md`.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project leadership directly via email (`<joaoanandalima@gmail.com>`). Violators risk immediate closure of pull requests, revocation of contributor status, or permanent ban from the KiloOS repository.
